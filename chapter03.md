# Chapter 3: Mitochondrial Dysfunction and Oxidative Stress

Mitochondrial toxicity is a central mechanism in FQAD.

## Key Evidence
Fluoroquinolones damage mtDNA, impair the electron transport chain (especially Complexes I and IV), and trigger excessive ROS production, creating a vicious cycle.

- Recent proteomics studies (2025) identify off-target binding to AIFM1, disrupting mitochondrial protein import.
- Delayed onset toxicity explained by persistent mtDNA depletion and reduced membrane potential (ΔΨm).

This leads to chronic fatigue, brain fog, myopathy, and multisystem symptoms. Supporting data from Kalghatgi et al. (2013) and updated reviews show bactericidal antibiotics like FQs uniquely induce mitochondrial ROS in mammalian cells.

Individual variability (e.g., pre-existing mitochondrial vulnerabilities) influences severity.
