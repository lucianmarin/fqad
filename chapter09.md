# Chapter 9: Principles of Recovery

No standardized cure exists; recovery focuses on supportive, mechanism-targeted care.

## Core Strategies
- Reduce oxidative stress.
- Support mitochondrial function.
- Cautiously replenish chelated ions.
- Promote detoxification.
- Pacing (avoid overexertion, NSAIDs initially).

Many improve over time; patience and avoiding re-exposure are crucial.
