# Chapter 10: Nutritional and Supplement Strategies

Evidence is largely from case reports, theoretical models, and patient communities; consult professionals.

## Recommendations
- Antioxidants → NAC/glutathione, alpha-lipoic acid, CoQ10, vitamins C/E.
- Minerals → Magnesium (antagonizes FQs), zinc; avoid excess iron/copper.
- Mitochondrial support → PQQ, L-carnitine, B vitamins (riboflavin, niacin).

Caveats: Unproven efficacy; individual responses vary.
