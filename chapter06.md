# Chapter 6: Other Systems and Multisystem Nature

FQAD is multisystem by definition (≥2 systems affected persistently).

## Affected Systems
- Cardiovascular — QT prolongation, aortic risks.
- Psychiatric — Mood disorders, suicidality.
- Sensory — Vision/hearing changes.
- Endocrine — Dysglycemia.

Symptoms persist long-term due to cumulative mitochondrial/oxidative damage and delayed onset.
