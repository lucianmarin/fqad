# Chapter 7: Diagnosis and Differential Diagnosis

No single test diagnoses FQAD; it relies on history and exclusion.

## Proposed Criteria
- Temporal link to FQ exposure.
- Multisystem disabling symptoms >30 days post-treatment (FDA operational definition).
- Pieper's criteria emphasize pathogenesis.

## Evaluation
- Rule out mimics — Fibromyalgia, Lyme, autoimmune diseases.
- Tests — Skin biopsy (small-fiber neuropathy), tendon MRI, organic acid testing (mitochondrial markers).

Early recognition is key to avoiding further triggers.
