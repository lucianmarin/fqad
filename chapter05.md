# Chapter 5: Neurological and Neuropsychiatric Effects

FQAD often involves peripheral neuropathy (small-fiber, autonomic) and central effects.

## Key Features
- Sensory/motor symptoms — Numbness, pain, weakness.
- CNS — Anxiety, insomnia, depression, rare psychosis.
- Mechanisms — GABAA receptor antagonism, glutamate imbalance, vagus nerve inhibition, mitochondrial neurotoxicity.

Gastrointestinal dysmotility emerges as a criterion linked to autonomic involvement.

FDA black box for irreversible neuropathy since 2013; studies show 47–83% increased risk, potentially permanent.
