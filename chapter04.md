# Chapter 4: Musculoskeletal and Connective Tissue Damage

Fluoroquinolones are well-known for causing tendinopathy, tendon rupture, and broader connective tissue issues.

## Mechanisms
- Upregulation of matrix metalloproteinases (MMPs).
- Reduced collagen synthesis.
- Oxidative damage to extracellular matrix.
- Chelation of magnesium essential for tendon integrity.

Risk of aortic aneurysm/dissection stems from similar collagen degradation in vascular tissue (FDA warning since 2018; multiple cohort studies show ~2x risk).

Recent data (2023–2025) reaffirm this, with higher incidence in older patients or those with hypertension/genetic conditions.
