# Chapter 2: The Science of Fluoroquinolone Toxicity

Fluoroquinolones are potent bactericidal antibiotics that target bacterial DNA gyrase and topoisomerase IV. In humans, off-target effects on similar mammalian enzymes (especially mitochondrial topoisomerase) lead to toxicity.

## Key Pathobiochemical Processes
- **Metal ion chelation** — FQs chelate divalent cations like Mg²⁺, Fe²⁺, Zn²⁺, disrupting enzyme function and transport.
- **Topoisomerase inhibition in mammalian cells** — Particularly affects mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA) replication and repair.
- **Genotoxicity and oxidative stress** — Increased ROS production, mtDNA damage, and epigenetic changes.

Evidence from comprehensive reviews (e.g., Hussen et al., 2024; Rusu et al., 2023) highlights long-term effects beyond antibacterial action, including CNS inhibition via GABAA receptors and collagen degradation via MMP upregulation.

Recent studies (2023–2025) confirm these mechanisms contribute to multisystem disability, with no reversal in some cases due to persistent cellular accumulation or irreversible damage.
